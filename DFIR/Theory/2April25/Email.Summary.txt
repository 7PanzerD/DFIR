Here are the **headers and sub-headers** from the "Email Forensics" module of the EC-Council's Digital Forensics Essentials course, along with **brief descriptors** of what is covered in each:

---

### **Module Title: Investigating Email Crimes**

---

### **Module Objectives**
- Understand the email system and communication process.
- Analyze the structure of an email message.
- Investigate email crimes and their methodology.

---

## **1. Understanding the Email System**

### **1.1 Introduction to an Email System**
- Overview of how email systems function using client-server architecture.

### **1.2 Components Involved in Email Communication**
- **Mail User Agent (MUA)** – e.g., Outlook, Thunderbird.
- **Mail Transfer Agent (MTA)** – Routes emails to destination.
- **Mail Delivery Agent (MDA)** – Delivers to recipient mailbox.
- **SMTP Server** – Handles outgoing emails.
- **POP3 Server / IMAP Server** – Handles incoming emails.

### **1.3 How Email Communication Works**
- Describes end-to-end flow of an email from sender to recipient using a real-world example.

---

## **2. Understanding the Parts of an Email Message**

### **2.1 Message Header**
- Fields like From, To, Subject, Date, MIME-Version, etc.

### **2.2 Message Body**
- Main content, can include HTML, text, images, etc.

### **2.3 Signature**
- Sender’s credentials or role appended at the end.

---

## **3. Understanding Email Crime Investigation and Its Steps**

### **3.1 Introduction to Email Crime Investigation**
- Classifies email crimes:
  - **By Sending Emails**: Spam, phishing, mail bombing, spoofing.
  - **Supported By Emails**: Identity fraud, cyberstalking, child abduction.

### **3.2 Steps to Investigate Email Crimes**
1. **Seizing the Computer and Email Accounts**
2. **Acquiring the Email Data**
   - From desktop clients like Outlook (.pst, .ost files).
   - Using tools like SysTools MailPro+.
3. **Examining Email Messages**
   - Focus on subject, sender, body, attachments.
4. **Retrieving Email Headers**
   - Different methods for Outlook, Outlook.com, Gmail.
5. **Analyzing Email Headers**
   - Elements: Timestamp, From, To, Message ID, MIME, SPF, DKIM, etc.
6. **Recovering Deleted Email Messages**
   - (Implied in the steps, though the full section may continue past the provided content.)

---

## **4. Investigating a Suspicious Email**

### **4.1 Examining the Email Message**
- Check for urgency, phishing tactics, or anomalies.

### **4.2 Checking the Link**
- Open links in a sandbox to observe redirection behavior.

### **4.3 Analyzing the Received Header Entries**
- Trace email back to origin via header chain.

### **4.4 Examine the Originating IP Address**
- Use IP tracing tools like whatismyipaddress.com.

### **4.5 Examine the Received-SPF Field**
- Analyze SPF validation status.

### **4.6 Check the Sender's Email Validity**
- Use tools like CentralOps Email Dossier.

### **4.7 Examine the Message ID**
- Check if message ID matches known legitimate domains.
